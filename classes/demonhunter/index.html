Demon Hunters are illidari warriors who sacrifice everything to turn demonic and cosmic void powers against Azeroth's enemies. In World of Warcraft: Midnight Season 2 (Patch 12.1), Demon Hunters excel in 3 specs: agile melee fel strikes with Havoc, fire-forged demon tanking with Vengeance, and devastating void-harnessing power with Devourer.
