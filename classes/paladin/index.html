WoW Midnight Paladin Overview (Patch 12.1)

Midnight Season 2
Armor Type Plate
Combat Roles Tank, Healer, DPS
Primary Resources Mana, Holy Power
Current Patch 12.1 (Season 2)

The Paladin is a hybrid plate class that uses the power of the Holy Light to smite enemies, protect companions, and heal injuries. In World of Warcraft: Midnight Season 2 (Patch 12.1), Paladins excel across all three combat roles: Tank (Protection), Healer (Holy), and Melee DPS (Retribution).
