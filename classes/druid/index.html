WoW Midnight Druid Overview (Patch 12.1)

Midnight Season 2
Armor Type Leather
Combat Roles Tank, Healer, Ranged & Melee DPS
Primary Resources Mana, Astral Power, Energy, Rage
Current Patch 12.1 (Season 2)

Druids are versatile shapeshifters connected deeply to the Emerald Dream and natural forces of Azeroth. In World of Warcraft: Midnight Season 2 (Patch 12.1), Druids can fill every combat role across their 4 specs: cosmic damage with Balance, feral bleeds with Feral, colossal bear tanking with Guardian, and rejuvenating restoration with Restoration.
