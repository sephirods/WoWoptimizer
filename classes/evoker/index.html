WoW Midnight Evoker Overview (Patch 12.1)

Midnight Season 2
Armor Type Mail
Combat Roles DPS, Healer, Support
Primary Resources Mana, Essence
Current Patch 12.1 (Season 2)

Evokers are draconic spellcasters created by Neltharion to wield the prismatic powers of all five dragonflights. In World of Warcraft: Midnight Season 2 (Patch 12.1), Evokers provide destructive breath magic with Devastation, time and nature healing with Preservation, and unique party damage amplification with Augmentation.
