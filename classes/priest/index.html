Priests are spiritual devotees who walk the fine line between the brilliance of the Holy Light and the cosmic whispers of the Void. In World of Warcraft: Midnight Season 2 (Patch 12.1), Priests provide preventative shields and damage-to-healing with Discipline, unmatched reactive throughput with Holy, and maddening shadow damage with Shadow.
